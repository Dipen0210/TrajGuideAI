Traffic Safety Rules & Guidelines

1. Following Distance (Space Headway):
   - Using the "2-second rule": The minimum safe space headway is 2.0 seconds under normal conditions.
   - At speeds > 20 m/s (~72 km/h), increase headway to 3.0 seconds.
   - Tailgating is defined as maintaining a headway of < 1.0 second.
   - Critical Safety Violation: Headway < 0.5 seconds at specific speed > 10 m/s.

2. Braking & Deceleration:
   - Comfortable braking is defined as deceleration < 2.0 m/s².
   - Hard braking is defined as deceleration between 2.0 m/s² and 4.0 m/s².
   - Emergency braking is defined as deceleration > 4.5 m/s².
   - Frequent hard braking indicates aggressive or inattentive driving.

3. Lane Keeping & Lateral Movement:
   - A vehicle is considered "stable" if lateral velocity is < 0.2 m/s.
   - A lane change event is characterized by significant lateral displacement (> 2.0m) and lateral velocity peaks.
   - "Weaving" or erratic driving is indicated by frequent lateral oscillations without completing a lane change.

4. Speed Limits (Contextual):
   - Highway assumed limit: 30 m/s (~108 km/h).
   - Urban assumed limit: 15 m/s (~54 km/h).
   - Overspeeding is defined as exceeding the contextual limit by > 10%.

5. Collision Risk (TTC):
   - Time-To-Collision (TTC) = Space Headway / Relative Velocity.
   - TTC < 1.5 seconds is an immediate collision warning.
   - TTC < 4.0 seconds warrants caution.
